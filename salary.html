⚾ Moneyball for Rugby — Unlocking World Rugby's Best Market Arbitrage

Market Arbitrage Index
Just as Billy Beane exploited baseball's market inefficiencies with On-Base Percentage, rugby union possesses a massive structural market inefficiency: Currency & Domestic Quota Disparities. European clubs (Top 14 / Premiership) pay premium salaries ($450k–$700k USD) for domestic squad players due to local player quotas (JIFF / EQP). Meanwhile, Fiji (Drua) and South Africa (URC Franchises) produce elite international talent at 15% to 40% of European cap costs.
