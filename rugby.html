Formula v3.0
S = (Ti×0.15 + Cd×0.25 + Fp×0.20 + Pc×0.15 + Ts×0.10 + Sd×0.15) × 10
Ti Individual Talent — competition tier of top players (URC/Top14/Super Rugby = ~9; domestic = ~4).
Cd Club Cohesion — squad concentration weighted by franchise quality (Leinster ×1.00; Japan League One ×0.55).
Fp Forward Pack Cohesion ⭐ Rugby-unique. Number of starting front 5 (props, hooker, locks) playing for the same top franchise.
Pc Playing Combination Index — National team time together: core XV stability + avg starter caps + head coach tenure.
Ts Tactical Style Cohesion — Clarity and consistency of tactical identity (Set Piece, Running, Kicking, Physicality).
Sd Squad Depth Index ✨ New v3.0. Bench quality (players 16–23) & matchday drop-off (e.g. South Africa's Bomb Squad).
